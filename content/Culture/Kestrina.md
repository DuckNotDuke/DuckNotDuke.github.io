The Kestrina are another people in the world, from across the [[Fog Ocean|Ocean]]. The arrived in Llimarn two years ago in 1726 c.e., but relationships quickly deteriorated. By 1727 c.e., they had taken over the [[Polaru Peninsula]], and set up multible forts such as [[Fornimar on Von]].

Interestingly, no Kestrina have been seen to be [[Hethsune]], and they have brought no [[Amshu]] animals with them. Kestrina have dark skin, hair, and eye color. Strangely, most high ranking officials such as generals or nobility have golden eyes. Local Llimarnic scholars have not been able to explain this oddity as of yet.

# Inspiration
My biggest inspiration for the Kestrina are the conquistadors and English invaders of the Americas.
