Around one and a half thousand years ago, an ocean of fog of unknown origin formed and rose over several years, covering the hole world in fog. This fog warped (see [[Origin of Llimarn's  Animals and Monsters]]) and chocked all that it covered. Many fled into the mountain peaks, massive and gradual, to a point were you can barely tell you are on a mountain.

The first 30 feet down of the fog from the shore is not immediately dangerous, killing you slightly slower than if you were drowning in water. The surface of the fog is perfectly opaque, and looks like a slow motion ocean made of cloud.Rivers flow right into the fog, and continue down to the [[True Sea]].
