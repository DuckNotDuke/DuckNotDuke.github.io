A Hethsune is a human who has physical characteristics of another mammalian spieces. An example is someone having the antlers of a deer. These characteristics are passed down through generations. A person is born with characteristics of no more than one other species. For the origins of Hethsunes, see [[Origin of Llimarn's  Animals and Monsters]].

Being Hethsune is common place, and, likely more commonplace that not being Hethsune. Being Hethsune is a mark that your ancestors even though they did not take instant warning and flee the fog, accepted [[Rin]] and [[Ram]] in the end. In a few extremest circles, it is considered holly to be "pure" human. Their are a few noble families that pride themselves in that they are untouched by the fog. Most people though, really don't care past superstition. 

There are some folktales about how you should never trust the word of a fox Hethsune, or that you should hold tight to your valuables when a raccoon Hethsune is around for they are surly a pickpocket. These tales are superstition, but, can be harmful, and cause people to have prejudice, which forces the people who have these prejudices to break the law, which reinforces the prejudice.
