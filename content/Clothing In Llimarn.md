# Overview
When  worldbuilding Llimarn clothing, I have had two main influences from medieval Great Britain and Asian (specifically Japanese and Chinese) clothing. Mid Llimarn outdoor clothing is made up of the following parts:
- Rainproof [[Alpaca]] cloaks
- [[Monsoon Grass|Polmap grass]] hats over [[Alpaca]] beanie like hats.
- Leather rain boots.
- A pair of flax shoes worn inside commonly kept in small leather bags.
# Hats
Hats are commonly worn in two pieces, a wool beanie like hat covered by a conical hat. The beanie is made of alpaca wool, and purpose is to keep peoples heads warm. The conical hat acts like a portable roof or awning.
