Monsoon grass is a blanket term for two different plants with a symbiotic relationship. These two plants are Polmap grass and Wesimar moss.

## Polmap Grass
Polmap grass is a knee to waist height grass that comes in deep greens. Polmap grass slows down water, as well as keeps soil from being washed away with their roots. The blades of Polmap grass have a waxy cover, which makes rain flow flow right of them, these root systems also leave holes for water flow.

Polmap grass has various uses, such as an edible (if bland) food. One use of Polmap grass is being used as a material in hats. For more on how a hat is made from Polmap grass is made, see [[Clothing In Llimarn]].

## Wesimar
Wesimar moss is a deep navy blue moss that grows at the base of Polmap grass. This moss is dark blue to photosynthesize any reflected light not photosynthesized by the above Polmap grass. Polmap grass gives Wesimar moss energy and a place protected from fast rains to grab onto, and in return, gives the Polmap grass nitrogen. This nitrogen comes from bacteria that love in the wet spongy structures of Wesimar moss, and these bacteria fix nitrogen from the air in return for getting to live on the Wesimar moss. Wesimar acts like a sponge for water, giving it a strange feeling to be walked on.  Wesimar moss is commonly used in teas and medicinal concoctions.
