Monsoon grass is a blanket term for two different plants with a symbiotic relationship. These two plants are Polmap grass and Wesmar moss.

## Polmap Grass
Polmap grass is a knee to waste height grass that comes in deep greens. Polmap grass slows down water, as well as keeps soil from being washed away with there roots. The blades of Polmap grass have a waxy cover, which makes rain flow flow right of them, these root systems also leave holes for water flow.

Polmap grass has various uses, such as an edible (if bland) food. One use of Polmap grass is being used as a material in hats. To make a Polmap grass hat, one cuts down a area of Polmap grass during [[The Four Seasons in Llamarn|Monsoon]], when they are at their tallest. These blades are weaved into a cone shape and this limp cone grass fabric is placed on a wooden frame to dry until [[The Four Seasons in Llamarn|Monsoon Fall]], at which point they are coated with a layer of tree sap until being attached to a wooden frame to make it so they can be worn.

## Wesimar
Wesimar moss is a deep navy blue moss that grows at the base of Polmap grass. This moss is dark blue to photosynthesize any refected light not photosynthesized by the above Polmap grass. Polmap grass gives Wesimar moss energy and a place protected from fast rains to grab onto, and in return, gives the Polmap grass nitrogen. This nitrogen comes from bacteria that love in the wet spongy structures of Wesimar grass, and these bacteria fix nitrogen from the air in return for getting to live on the Wesimar moss. Wesimar acts like a sponge for water, giving it a strange feeling to be walked on.
