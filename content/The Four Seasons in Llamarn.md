Four Seasons (Unique names still to come):
- Monsoon Fall: Temperatures rise, going from Monsoon's cold rainy weather around 40 degrees Fahrenheit to Drizzle's 80. Rain levels drop, and the planting season begins.
- Drizzle: A season must similar to summer. During drizzle temperature hovers around 80 degrees Fahrenheit. Rain is sparser than than the rest of the year, being an on and off drizzle.
- Monsoon Rise: It becomes rainier and colder, going from Drizzles 80 degrees Fahrenheit to Monsoon's 40 degrees Fahrenheit. The harvest season begins.
- Monsoon: Most trees have lost their leaves, as the rain pelts through the leaves, even waxy ones. Rain becomes most common and heavier, and temperatures drop to 40 degrees Fahrenheit. Farming is not done during this season.
