# The Story of Blessed Rain and Light
Llimarn and the surrounding  [[Fog Ocean]] are populated by many strange creatures, such as the [[Eagoosel]]. It is an open debate among scholars of how these creatures came to be, but religious texts show one main story.

The story goes that when the fog rose and covered the world, it warped the creatures it enveloped, twisting and combining these creatures into horrible monstrosities. Survivors on the peaks all prayed to [[Rin]] and [[Ram]]. The sibling gods had pity and blessed  the land with holly light and rain. All that was touched by this light and rain were purified, but stayed warped. All things quickly grew and changed to live in this new world. This holly rain and light went for 10 days and nights. All  creature who remained in the fog stayed monstrosities, while those that embraced Rin and Ram were blessed and forgiven.

# How Humans Were Effected
Many humans were fool-hearty and did not flee to the mountains from the rich but greedy kingdoms of old. These people were warped into half-wit, eyeless, grey skinned, cannibalistic monsters after generations of breathing in the warping fog. These monstrosities are referred to simply as the "Ancients."

Those who fled were not left untouched. Many of the people who live in [[Llimarn]] today's ancestors experienced enough exposure to the fog to be warped in small ways. Many humans are born with physical characteristics of another mammalian species. These characteristics are passed down through family lines, and a person can only have characteristics from one other species. "Pure" Llimarnic humans  still exist, but are becoming rarer and rarer. These ancestors did not become [[Ancients]] as they did not get exposed to as much fog as the [[Ancients]], and their for, could still embraced the healing light and rain.
