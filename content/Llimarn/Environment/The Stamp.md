---
World: Llimarn
---
The Stamp is a large flightless domestic bird that stands around eight feet tall. They are completely flightless, though they are swift footed. People in Llimarn use Stamps as real word peoples use horses. Stamps work best in warfare as light cavalierly, though there are a few  breeds that are bred to be heavy cavalierly. Male stamps have blue feathers with brown highlights, while females have the opposite.
