These are the suffixes/prefixes I use to help make names for places.
# Llimarn
1. Shu/Su: Means middle man, or a in between location.
2. Von: River.
3. Imar: Body of water of fog, but usually not a river.
4. Polm: Mountain or Peak.
5. Rin: Light, warmth, and the sun.
6. Hest/Thest: Settlement.
7. Forn: Castle or fort.
8. Nil: Hole.
9. Ram: Rain.
10. Nilpolm: A mine. A combination of Nil and Polm.
# The People From Across the Sea
1. Ku: Means middle man, or a in between location.
2. Forn: A castle or fort.
3. Nol: Mountian, wall, or highpoint.
4. Rin: Light, warmth, and the sun.
5. Von: River or body of water.
6. Mer: Fog.
7. Kest: Settlement.
### Banned Combos
1. Th, as in non voiced version, as in theater. Instead, use "k," so theater would become keater.
