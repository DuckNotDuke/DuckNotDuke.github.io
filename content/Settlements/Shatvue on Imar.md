# Landmarks
## The Leaning Watchman
Off into the sea right off of Shatvue on Imar's shore is a great statue standing 100 feet tall, with only its mid chest and higher above the [[Fog Ocean]]. It is leaning a bit to the side, and looks away from the town towards the west. This statue is made of grey mossed over stone, and appears to be in plate armor. They stand tall holding a sword point down in front of them. Birds now roost on this statue, once likely grand before the fog sea enveloped the world, and half of its head (the left side) has fallen off.
### The Legend of the Halfheaded Knight
Legend has it that on the knight of the full moon, from the fog comes a knight in rusted iron armor with half of his head missing. He is said to hunt the town for the person who in a duel unfaraly won and mangled this knights face. Who ever he comes apon, may it be the person he trully wants revenge on or not, he challenges them to a duel that the other can not refuse. In that dule, he will do to that person what happened to him and then continue his hunt until day break, at which point he returns into the fog.

The locals tell of how several years ago the ruler of the town ordered for the statue to be turned into a lighthouse. The architect hired even with the local's warning went on a single person boat to visit and survy the statue. It was a lightly foggy day, so no one saw what happened to this architect. After hours, he did not return. Hours turned to days. At the end of the month on the day of the full moon, people barred their doors and drew their blinds. They drifted to sleep feeling discomforted in the rumor that the knight had got to the architect. The next morning, a neat box had appeared on the ruler of this town's doorstep. In it, half of the architect head, now just a skull and half of his pair of glasses.
