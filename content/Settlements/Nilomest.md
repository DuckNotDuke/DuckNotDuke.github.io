Nilomest as a village formed around an iron mine, which was resonantly closed due to a hooked horror that was removed bu a group of adventures. The local master blacksmith also sells in this villages black market his "special" mushrooms (100% guaranteed to NOT give you hallucinations).
# Additional Information
## Lay of the Land
Nilomest resides high up in the mountain, at the base of a cliff, which the mines enter into.
## Tone/Vibe
This village has a Boom town vibe. It has cold dusty colors, and a desperate feeling.
## Roof and Meal
The houses are quickly made and kinda scrapped together. Lots of use of rusted iron. A meal is usually a small bowl of rice and bland soup.
